Privacy Policy

Lost & Found Desk · Last updated: 27 October 2026

We do not collect data. This app shows no ads, uses no analytics, requires no account, and does not track you.

What is stored?

Game progress (unlocked days, coins, office decor, sound setting) is kept only in on-device local storage (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages (4+). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

There are no third-party SDKs, ad networks, or analytics services.
